## UK data protection test for US contractual transfers

The [ICO permits use of the UK government’s DSIT analysis](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/uk-gdpr-guidance-and-resources/international-transfers/completing-a-transfer-risk-assessment/completing-a-tra-using-the-uk-government-s-analysis-us/) for the US country assessment. It addresses access by third parties outside the contract, including public bodies, and whether US law makes the contractual protections difficult to enforce. This is a proportionate starting point for the documented US flows. Apply it only to US processing and reassess any other onward destination. Review again if the guidance changes or the provider, country, data categories or retention settings change.

The residual risk depends on the data sent and how long it is accessible. Internal references avoid sharing account credentials, but note text can identify the viewer or someone else and reveal health, religion or other protected information. A small invited audience reduces volume and leaves the consequences of a single disclosure unchanged. Retention, downstream access and deletion matter for both AI providers. A provider’s published Addendum is evidence of its terms. Confirm applicability to the account, processing chain and stated scope. Record any additional measures and the controller’s decision before relying on it. [ICO data protection test](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/uk-gdpr-guidance-and-resources/international-transfers/completing-a-transfer-risk-assessment/what-s-a-transfer-risk-assessment-tra/)

## Early access decision

Rating notes stay available in early access. They are central to how Glutton learns what viewers liked and disliked about films.

Focus safeguards on that workflow. Tell viewers beside the notes field that Glutton uses AI to analyse their notes and ask them to keep notes about the film and leave out identifying or sensitive information about themselves or others. Establish how to handle incidental sensitive text, including how a viewer can remove it and how Glutton can request deletion from providers where applicable. The prompt helps reduce accidental disclosures. Any personal information entered in a note can still be processed.

Confirm TypeSafe's handling of incidental sensitive content and request zero data retention. Verify OpenAI's retention settings and its handling of the same content. Record the controller's assessment of the safeguards for the flow, including any applicable Article 9 condition where special category data is processed. Zero data retention reduces how long a provider holds content after processing. [ICO special category guidance](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/uk-gdpr-guidance-and-resources/lawful-basis/a-guide-to-lawful-basis/special-category-data/)

## Accidental sensitive details in rating notes

OpenAI’s current DPA Schedule 1 explicitly anticipates sensitive information unexpectedly included in unstructured user data. This describes the scope of processing and does not promise automatic detection, redaction or expedited deletion. Glutton’s Responses API requests set `store: false`. Standard abuse monitoring logs can still contain prompts or responses for up to 30 days, subject to legal and safety exceptions. If a viewer reports an incident, use OpenAI support and the DPA’s rights assistance process to ask whether the specific request and any retained copies can be located and erased. Early removal from abuse monitoring logs is unconfirmed. [OpenAI DPA](https://openai.com/policies/data-processing-addendum/) · [API retention](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/guides/your-data)

TypeSafe’s DPA Schedule I lists sensitive data as not applicable and does not specify an exception or handling process for incidental disclosure in free text. Its standard retention has no published fixed period. TypeSafe directs customers to Privacy & data support for deletion and zero data retention. Ask support to confirm whether incidental UK special category data is covered, how to identify and delete an affected request and its retained copies, and whether deletion reaches subprocessors and backups. [TypeSafe DPA](https://typesafe.ai/legal/data-processing) · [Retention](https://help.typesafe.ai/articles/5974925716) · [Privacy support](https://help.typesafe.ai/articles/8737694518-who-should-i-reach-out-to-with-support-requests)

Keep rating notes available. When a report arrives, use timestamps and request IDs if available when contacting a provider. Avoid copying the sensitive text into a support ticket. Check for historical ratings, stored assessment payloads and profile evidence that could retain or resend the old text after an edit.

# Wikipedia coverage and film quality counts

A read-only snapshot of the 500-film development catalogue compared word counts in the Wikipedia narrative sections passed to the latest completed quality evaluation with accepted Registry film quality counts. All 500 films had narrative text and a completed evaluation, and the accepted counts matched the selected counts. This sample cannot compare missing versus available Wikipedia context: the importer requires usable narrative context.

Spearman rank correlation, averaging tied ranks: **0.136**. Shorter narrative text is a weak signal for fewer assigned qualities. There is substantial overlap, and word count says little about whether relevant information is missing. The vocabulary, assessment policy and category caps also affect counts. The seed is a selected horror catalogue; the result does not establish that editing an article improves assignment.

## Next check

Use low counts to shortlist films for internal review, including both short and long articles. Compare them with ordinary-count films and label cases as missing source information, existing information that did not match the vocabulary or rules, or legitimately few applicable qualities. Review before offering any public list. Use Registry film quality counts, which do not depend on whether a viewer rated the film. If confirmed source gaps recur, invite users to share article and revision links through a feedback form so maintainers can review the change before refreshing the source or reassessing.

## Discovering missing qualities

Start with a monthly manual review once there is enough explicitly shared feedback. Invite viewers to opt in to sharing a note for product improvement. Review a small sample across viewers and films, looking for the same concrete characteristic described independently. Compare it with the existing vocabulary and accepted qualities for those films, then record whether the issue is missing source evidence, a definition or matching gap, or a candidate new quality. Keep the working list to paraphrased patterns and counts rather than copying private notes into it. For promising candidates, collect a few positive examples and near misses, test against existing definitions, and review whether the change would improve recommendations before adopting it. Keep this process separate from automatic taste profile changes.

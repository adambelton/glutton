# Working film-quality list

235 provisional candidates, grouped for navigation. IDs, names, categories, definitions and exclusions are unchanged from the latest six-film test. Category order does not imply priority. All qualities/categories start with equal base treatment. See [requirements](../film_quality_requirements.md).

## Subgenres

| Quality / ID | Definition | Exclusion |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Supernatural haunting · `supernatural_haunting` | A ghost or haunting causes danger, fear or disturbance in the story. | A ghost costume, a title or a metaphor for a troubled past does not establish a haunting. |
| Possession horror · `possession_horror` | An invading entity takes control of a person's body or actions. | Unusual behaviour, hallucination or replacement by a separate duplicate does not by itself establish invading control. |
| Psychological horror · `psychological_horror` | Mental disturbance, unreliable perception or internal psychological conflict is a source of the film's horror. | Fear caused by a known external danger alone does not establish a psychological horror mechanism. |
| Body horror · `body_horror` | Bodily violation, mutation or loss of bodily integrity is presented as a source of horror. | An injury mentioned in a synopsis or the mere appearance of a creature does not establish horror about bodily integrity. |
| Folk horror · `folk_horror` | Folk beliefs, local traditions or community rituals are a source of horror. | A rural setting alone does not establish folk horror. Invented folklore need not be an authentic historical practice. |
| Cosmic horror · `cosmic_horror` | An incomprehensible nonhuman reality threatens human significance, knowledge or identity. | A god, monster, alien or global danger alone does not establish incomprehensibility or human insignificance. |
| Slasher horror · `slasher_horror` | A killer repeatedly stalks and attacks a succession of victims. | One attack or a murder without stalking a succession of victims does not establish this pattern. |
| Creature feature · `creature_feature` | A nonhuman creature poses a physical threat in the story. | A harmless creature, a metaphor or a creature mentioned only in a title does not establish the physical threat. |
| Zombie horror · `zombie_horror` | Reanimated or zombie-like humans threaten people in the story. | A dead body alone or a figurative description of someone as a zombie does not establish a zombie threat. |
| Vampire horror · `vampire_horror` | Vampirism is a source of danger or horror in the story. | Blood loss or a vampire reference alone does not establish a vampiric threat. |
| Werewolf horror · `werewolf_horror` | A human transforms into a wolf-like predator that is a source of danger or horror. | A wolf attack or a comparison with a wolf does not establish human transformation. |
| Witchcraft horror · `witchcraft_horror` | Witches or deliberate magical practice cause danger or horror. | An accusation of witchcraft without supported magical practice or a witch threat does not establish this mechanism. |
| Occult horror · `occult_horror` | Hidden supernatural knowledge or esoteric practice is involved in the film's threat. | A secret with no supernatural or esoteric connection does not establish occult horror. |
| Religious horror · `religious_horror` | Sacred beliefs, religious evil or spiritual conflict are a source of horror. | Religious clothing, a building or a character's affiliation alone does not establish religious horror. |
| Science-fiction horror · `science_fiction_horror` | A scientific or extraterrestrial premise produces danger or horror. | A scientist's presence or ordinary technology alone does not establish a science-fiction premise. |
| Ecological horror · `ecological_horror` | Ecological disturbance or a hostile natural world produces danger or horror. | An outdoor setting alone does not establish ecological horror. |
| Survival horror · `survival_horror` | Characters struggle to stay alive against an immediate lethal threat. | A reference to possible death without a depicted struggle to survive does not establish survival horror. |
| Home-invasion horror · `home_invasion_horror` | Intruders enter a domestic refuge and threaten its occupants. | A threat outside a home or an invited visitor with no threatening intrusion does not establish home invasion. |
| Revenge horror · `revenge_horror` | Retaliation for an earlier wrong causes threatening or horrific actions. | Unrelated violence after an earlier wrong does not establish retaliation. |
| Horror comedy · `horror_comedy` | The film combines horror with comic treatment or situations. | A character smiling or an isolated quoted joke without comic treatment of the film's events does not establish horror comedy. |
| Gothic horror · `gothic_horror` | Decay, haunted histories or Gothic spaces and sensibilities contribute to the film's horror. | An old building alone does not establish a Gothic treatment. |
| Found-footage horror · `found_footage_horror` | Recordings presented as recovered or discovered material form part of the film's storytelling. | A recording played as an ordinary prop does not establish a recovered-footage presentation. |
| Screenlife horror · `screenlife_horror` | Events are presented to the audience through computer or phone interfaces. | Characters using devices in ordinary camera shots does not establish interface-based presentation. |
| Anthology horror · `anthology_horror` | The film is composed of several distinct horror stories. | A backstory, flashback or change of viewpoint within one story does not alone establish an anthology. |
| Backwoods horror · `backwoods_horror` | An isolated rural or backcountry environment exposes characters to the film's threat. | A brief journey through countryside without a connection to the threat does not establish this setting mechanism. |
| Urban horror · `urban_horror` | Conditions or social spaces of city life contribute to the film's horror. | A city address alone does not establish a connection between urban life and the horror. |
| Apocalyptic horror · `apocalyptic_horror` | Societal collapse or a world-ending catastrophe is depicted or described within the story. | A local disaster alone does not establish societal collapse or a world-ending threat. |
| Epidemic horror · `epidemic_horror` | Disease or infection spreads through a population and creates danger. | One infected person without spread or an established outbreak does not establish an epidemic. |
| Erotic horror · `erotic_horror` | Sexual desire is linked to the film's horror or danger. | Nudity or an intimate relationship alone does not establish the desire-horror connection. |
| Medical horror · `medical_horror` | Medical treatment, institutions or experimentation produce danger or horror. | A hospital location or a doctor's presence alone does not establish medical horror. |
## Tropes and situations

| Quality / ID | Definition | Exclusion |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Remote isolation · `remote_isolation` | Distance from assistance limits characters' ability to obtain help against danger. | A remote place name alone does not establish difficulty obtaining help. |
| Confining interiors · `confining_interiors` | Enclosed indoor spaces limit escape or action. | An indoor scene alone does not establish restricted escape or movement. |
| Ritual survival rules · `ritual_survival_rules` | Following specified ritual rules enables characters to navigate or survive a threat. | A ritual performed without survival conditions or consequences does not establish survival rules. |
| Bargain with an entity · `bargain_with_an_entity` | A negotiated exchange with a supernatural being has terms that affect the participants. | An offering without an exchange or agreement does not establish a bargain. |
| Secret cult · `secret_cult` | An organised hidden group serves a dangerous belief or entity. | Secrecy or conspiracy alone does not establish a shared cultic belief or devotion. |
| Doppelganger · `doppelganger` | A double or imitation of a person creates danger or uncertainty. | A resemblance with no identity-double role does not establish a doppelganger. |
| Concealed infection · `concealed_infection` | An infected or assimilated person's condition is hidden from others. | A known infection or an unrelated secret does not establish concealed infection. |
| Corrupted child · `corrupted_child` | A child becomes a threatening agent through corruption or supernatural influence. | A child in danger or a malicious adult described as childlike does not establish a corrupted child. |
| Inherited curse · `inherited_curse` | A supernatural affliction passes through a family or lineage. | A family misfortune without a supernatural transmission does not establish an inherited curse. |
| Hostile landscape · `hostile_landscape` | Terrain or environmental conditions increase the danger faced by characters. | A landscape description without an adverse effect on characters does not establish a hostile landscape. |
| Stalking pursuit · `stalking_pursuit` | A predator repeatedly follows or hunts a particular target. | One unexpected attack without following or hunting does not establish stalking pursuit. |
| Contagious curse · `contagious_curse` | A supernatural threat passes between people through a described mechanism. | An ordinary infection or several unrelated supernatural attacks do not establish curse transmission. |
| Last survivor · `last_survivor` | Only one member of the threatened group is left alive in the story's stated outcome. | A source mentioning one survivor without establishing the fate of the rest does not establish a last survivor. |
| Siege · `siege` | An opposing force surrounds or contains people in a location they defend. | Confinement without a besieging force or a defended position does not establish a siege. |
| Grotesque transformation · `grotesque_transformation` | A body is shown or described changing into a grotesque form. | A pre-existing unusual appearance without transformation does not establish grotesque transformation. |
## Narrative devices

| Quality / ID | Definition | Exclusion |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Slow-burn pacing · `slow_burn_pacing` | The completed film's pacing is described as gradual or slow-building. | Chronological escalation, a decades-long plot or a long rough cut does not establish slow screen pacing. |
| Relentless pacing · `relentless_pacing` | The completed film's pacing is described as rapid or unremitting. | Many plot events or a high body count alone do not establish rapid or unremitting screen pacing. |
| Investigation-led narrative · `investigation_led_narrative` | Characters' systematic investigation moves the story forward. | A single question or accidental discovery without investigation does not establish an investigation-led narrative. |
| Whodunit structure · `whodunit_structure` | The narrative presents a mystery about which person committed a crime. | A known perpetrator or a mystery about a non-criminal event does not establish a whodunit. |
| Hidden-identity mystery · `hidden_identity_mystery` | Suspense concerns who or what a character really is. | A hidden name without identity-related suspense does not establish a hidden-identity mystery. |
| Unreliable narration · `unreliable_narration` | A narrator's account of events is misleading or unreliable. | A character who lacks information without misleading narration does not establish an unreliable narrator. |
| Uncertain perception · `uncertain_perception` | A viewpoint character sees or hears events whose occurrence or accuracy is called into question. | Uncertainty about another person's identity, motive or danger without unreliable seeing or hearing does not establish uncertain perception. |
| Nonlinear chronology · `nonlinear_chronology` | The film presents events out of chronological order. | A synopsis retelling events out of order or a character recalling something without a presented time shift does not establish nonlinear chronology. |
| Time loop · `time_loop` | Characters experience a literal repeated period of time. | Similar events on different days without literal temporal repetition do not establish a time loop. |
| Repeated ordeal · `repeated_ordeal` | A similar dangerous procedure or ordeal recurs with variations. | Several unrelated dangers or one ordeal lasting a long time do not establish a repeated ordeal. |
| Dramatic irony · `dramatic_irony` | The audience is given information relevant to a character's situation that the character lacks. | Characters knowing different things does not establish that the audience knows more than the affected character. |
| Restricted viewpoint · `restricted_viewpoint` | The audience's access to events and information is limited to one character's experience. | Having one protagonist alone does not establish restricted presentation of information. |
| Alternating viewpoints · `alternating_viewpoints` | The narrative alternates among different characters' perspectives. | A conversation or a camera cut between speakers alone does not establish alternating narrative viewpoints. |
| Concealed monster · `concealed_monster` | The film withholds the threat's physical form before revealing it or keeping it unseen. | A synopsis omitting the creature's appearance does not establish that the film conceals it. |
| Delayed explanation · `delayed_explanation` | The narrative postpones an explanation of events that it has presented as unexplained. | A synopsis explaining events late does not establish that the film delays the explanation. |
| Unexplained mechanism · `unexplained_mechanism` | The mechanism of a threat is left unexplained by the film. | A source omitting an explanation does not establish that the completed film leaves it unexplained. |
| Identity-revelation twist · `identity_revelation_twist` | An identity revelation changes how earlier events are understood. | An identity disclosed without revising the interpretation of earlier events does not establish an identity-revelation twist. |
| Perspective-reversal twist · `perspective_reversal_twist` | A revelation changes which account or perspective earlier events are understood through. | New information that only continues the story without changing its earlier interpretation does not establish a perspective reversal. |
| False ending · `false_ending` | An apparent conclusion is followed by renewed threat or a reversal that reopens the story. | A further scene after a conclusion without renewed threat or reversal does not establish a false ending. |
| Real-time structure · `real_time_structure` | The film's presented action unfolds approximately continuously in real time. | A short story duration or a synopsis without time markers does not establish real-time presentation. |

| Conformity and dissent · `conformity_and_dissent` | Characters face pressure to conform or consequences for resisting group expectations. | Simple agreement without pressure or dissent does not establish conformity and dissent. |

## Ironies and reversals

| Quality / ID | Definition | Exclusion |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Protector becomes threat · `protector_becomes_threat` | A person or force expected to protect someone instead becomes a source of danger to them. | An unrelated attacker with no prior protective role does not establish protector becomes threat. |
| Sanctuary becomes trap · `sanctuary_becomes_trap` | A place sought as protection instead confines people with a threat. | A dangerous location never sought as refuge does not establish sanctuary becomes trap. |
| Survival spreads danger · `survival_spreads_danger` | An action that preserves someone's life spreads a threat to others. | Survival followed by unrelated harm does not establish survival spreading danger. |
| Defeating evil creates evil · `defeating_evil_creates_evil` | An attempt to defeat a threat creates a new threatening evil. | A later unrelated threat or the original threat surviving does not establish this causal creation. |
| Desired reward causes ruin · `desired_reward_causes_ruin` | Obtaining or pursuing a desired reward brings about a character's downfall. | A reward and later misfortune without a causal link do not establish this reversal. |
| Knowledge increases vulnerability · `knowledge_increases_vulnerability` | Learning sought information makes the learner more vulnerable. | Learning about existing danger without increasing vulnerability does not establish this reversal. |
| Denial enables harm · `denial_enables_harm` | Refusal to acknowledge danger enables further harm. | A mistaken belief without refusal or a connection to further harm does not establish denial enabling harm. |
| Loyalty protects the predator · `loyalty_protects_the_predator` | Loyalty to an apparent ally shields a threatening antagonist. | An antagonist escaping without protection arising from loyalty does not establish this reversal. |
| Suspicion harms the innocent · `suspicion_harms_the_innocent` | Suspicion of a threat causes harm to someone not established as that threat. | Suspicion without harm or harm to an established threat does not establish this quality. |
| Cruelty undermines survival · `cruelty_undermines_survival` | Cruel treatment of others damages the cruel character's or group's ability to survive. | Cruelty and later danger without a causal relation do not establish this reversal. |
| Compassion breaks the threat's hold · `compassion_breaks_the_threat_s_hold` | Care or compassion weakens a threat's hold over someone. | Compassion accompanying a rescue without weakening the threat does not establish this mechanism. |
| Monster shows greater humanity · `monster_shows_greater_humanity` | A monster demonstrates more compassion or moral consideration than a human counterpart. | A sympathetic monster without a comparison in treatment does not establish greater humanity than a human counterpart. |
| Respectability conceals evil · `respectability_conceals_evil` | A socially respectable appearance or role conceals wrongdoing or monstrosity. | A respectable character who is openly known as dangerous does not establish concealment by respectability. |
| Innocence conceals malice · `innocence_conceals_malice` | An apparently innocent person is revealed as deliberately malicious. | A person causing accidental harm or being falsely accused does not establish concealed malice. |
| Cure worsens the affliction · `cure_worsens_the_affliction` | An intended cure makes the underlying affliction worse. | A failed cure leaving the condition unchanged does not establish worsening. |
| Repression strengthens the threat · `repression_strengthens_the_threat` | Suppressing an experience or feeling strengthens the threat. | Repression and a growing threat without a causal link do not establish this mechanism. |
| Control produces chaos · `control_produces_chaos` | An effort to establish control makes events more uncontrollable. | An unsuccessful attempt at control without increased disorder does not establish this reversal. |
| Victory conceals defeat · `victory_conceals_defeat` | An apparent victory is revealed to include a defeat or loss. | An openly partial victory without a concealed defeat does not establish this reversal. |
| Selfish character makes a sacrifice · `selfish_character_makes_a_sacrifice` | A character established as selfish later makes a self-sacrifice. | A helpful act without personal sacrifice or an earlier selfish portrayal does not establish this reversal. |
| Escape perpetuates the cycle · `escape_perpetuates_the_cycle` | Escape from immediate danger continues a harmful cycle for others. | A threat merely remaining alive under containment does not establish that escape perpetuates a cycle. |

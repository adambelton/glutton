# Film qualities, sources and reassessment

Status: Current direction and open design questions, 3 November 2026  
Owner: Film knowledge

This replaces the former film-analysis dossier requirements. Owner decision: remove FilmAnalysis and AnalysisQuality. The model roles are Quality, QualityCategory, Film and FilmQuality (the film–quality link). Direct current film qualities/source context and persisted categories are implemented; the generated-analysis path is retired. Original creation migrations express the schema directly. Production deployment remains separate.

## Purpose and vocabulary

Glutton owns useful descriptions of a viewer's relationship with horror. Vocabulary membership should have a plausible relationship to enjoyment or avoidance; that editorial hypothesis must not become a film-level centrality or importance gate. Detectability, trope occurrence or reuse across films alone is insufficient. “Humans are the real monsters” expresses a consequential moral outlook; a possessed prop or a broken telephone may simply describe plot mechanics.

The [working vocabulary](film_qualities/MASTER_LIST.md) has 235 provisional candidates after reviewing the original approximately 250. It is a starting point, not a final taxonomy or a set of validated preference predictors. The retained baseline supplies explicit definitions and exclusions. Preference relevance remains an editorial hypothesis, not guidance sent to JEV. Selection uses **a maximum stored against each category**, rather than one global maximum of 20. Category limits can differ; they are ceilings, never quotas. Initial defaults are listed below and remain editable. Avoid padding and redundant associations; a cap alone does not validate additive scoring or prevent correlated evidence from being counted repeatedly.

Qualities and **quality categories** are editable product data. Categories organise qualities; they are not hard-coded dimensions, behavioural subclasses or mutually exclusive options. Their names do not imply built-in scoring priority. New categories must not require deployment. Keep a quality's identity when its meaning is unchanged, including category moves. Quality names are immutable after creation; definitions/exclusions may be tuned within the same concept. Category names remain editable. A new meaning requires an explicit new concept rather than repurposing an existing name. Changed meaning, splits and merges need explicit interpretation/reassessment rather than silently reusing an identifier. Retire referenced qualities instead of destroying the meaning of historical score records.

Category maxima and weights are persisted product data, with every initial weight set to 1. The initial assignment baseline is >=60% contextual support within each category ceiling. The [selection-policy experiment](research/quality_selection_policy.md) compared uniform ceilings to study effects; it did not prescribe identical category defaults. There are no category minimums, automatic changes to accepted associations or user-facing weighting controls. Selection/scoring execution and admin forms remain to build; category spread alone does not establish fair scores.

Persisted categories organise the Quality vocabulary, and the former analysis compatibility is removed. Initial selection is implemented; scoring remains separately owned work. Baseline maxima are subgenres 2; tropes/situations 5; narrative devices 3; themes 4; ironies/reversals 2; character/social dynamics 3; presentation/intensity 4; emotional tone/experience 3; resolution/aftermath 2, with every weight 1. [YAML category seed data](../config/quality_categories.yml) supplies defaults for the one-time initial load; the [vocabulary seed](../config/quality_vocabulary.yml) references category names. Categories and qualities use database IDs for associations, without separate keys. The 60% support gate is the implemented initial-selection baseline.

## Equal starting weights and personal preference

Owner decision, 28 October 2026: all admitted qualities begin with equal base treatment, and every quality category has the same baseline multiplier (conceptually 1). Do not add a global multiplier based on a quality's theme, category, narrative centrality, JEV importance answer or model confidence. This does not add per-quality intrinsic weights or establish an implemented scoring formula.

Film-wide centrality is different from influence on an individual's enjoyment. The test favoured greed because its question explicitly rewarded central conflict and moral outlook. Minimal dialogue is a viewing-form characteristic that can matter more to a particular viewer. It remains eligible on the same basis as a theme when its definition and source support are satisfied. A meaningful quality need not be the central plot concern or occupy many screen minutes.

The initial workflow should identify source-supported qualities without an additional defining-importance gate. Retain supported candidates for inspection; do not discard them merely because JEV considers them secondary. Initial selection uses the agreed support/category-cap baseline. Support probability remains confidence in evidence, not preference value; the chosen arithmetic does not validate recommendation scoring. Review redundancy and preference usefulness without imposing category quotas or a theme-over-presentation hierarchy. Future weights cannot recover a useful quality that was excluded from the film beforehand.

Equal base treatment does not make every user's derived contribution identical. Actual rating strength, explicit appreciation/dislike/indifference and the agreed evidence classifications can still produce different contributions under Ratings rules. Keep those user-derived distinctions separate from an additional intrinsic quality/category weight.

Admin tuning should expose two independent controls: a category maximum governing film-quality selection, and a category weight governing its contribution to scoring. Category maxima may differ without changing the initial equal-weight baseline. Changing a maximum affects future assignment/suggestion policy; it must not silently alter accepted film associations. A weight change affects scoring policy, not JEV presence judgments or film membership; specify its recalculation/versioning behaviour in separately owned scoring tasks, preserving historical explanations. These controls have different ownership effects and must not be bundled into a cross-domain mutation. Admins control them initially; user controls and automated learning remain later possibilities.

## Source material and film updates

Wikipedia supplies maintained narrative/context material. Retain the relevant page identity, source revision and attribution/licence with imported material. BFI may supplement useful gaps; screenplay processing and model-led research are not prerequisites for a useful seed. Initial-release improvements to quality meanings, evidence and film associations are admin-only; community editing is deferred. Plot corrections belong on Wikipedia, rather than in a second Glutton plot editor.

A Wikipedia page update is a **source refresh**, not an accepted change to a film's qualities. Build manual TMDB/Wikipedia refresh after seed loading, admin content viewing and manual editing. This first refresh step updates source data/provenance and reports outcomes without requiring a JEV check. Next, wire a follow-up JEV check into that flow (including a deliberately requested check on unchanged source content), comparing the proposed category-limited set with current accepted qualities. Show additions/removals and preserve manual corrections until explicit admin application. A successful sync and failed/stale check are separate outcomes; unchanged checks do not rescore. Keep input/policy provenance inspectable. Scheduled sync cadence/check policy remains separately scoped later work; no callback or scheduler is a prerequisite for the first usable seed/admin path.

A source record/version or a minimal current assignment revision used for freshness does not require a permanent archive of every former film-quality set. Source provenance, editorial audit and historical scoring are different needs; retain each only for an identified purpose.

Source descriptions must distinguish depicted events, attributed responses, supported interpretation and production intentions. A script does not prove finished-film sound or imagery; an unmade plan does not establish an achieved effect. Lack of evidence is unknown, not absence. A critic's response is not a universal viewer response or evidence that a future user will enjoy the film. Copies of one account are not independent corroboration.

## JEV questions and request packing

JEV is the chosen engine. It evaluates supplied context and explicit quality meanings; it should not do heavy research or invent the vocabulary. Film matching, expression interpretation and preference direction/strength are separate judgments. A Noul probability of film presence is not a preference weight or a score contribution.

### Neutral matching baseline

Owner clarification, 28 October 2026: the initial JEV evaluation asks whether each defined quality is supported in the supplied film context. Do not ask it to favour qualities that are important, influential, defining, desirable, useful for recommendations or likely to affect enjoyment. Do not supply category priorities or instructions to prefer themes over presentation. Such guidance requires a deliberate, separately evaluated experiment with evidence before adoption.

The [retained matching contract](film_qualities/matching.json) asks: **“Do the supplied source_paragraphs support [quality] in this film?”** It supplies each quality's definition and exclusion, together with the shared source-evidence policy. The 235 current [definitions](film_qualities/vocabulary.json) are the ones used in the latest six-film test. They retain concept-specific meaning (for example, duration in “sustained dread”) without a blanket importance hurdle. They remain provisional and can evolve; known semantic ambiguity is not a reason to block the seed or endlessly fit these six films.

Keep all per-quality support probabilities available for inspection before category-limit selection. The cap remains a product constraint, not a model instruction to invent a quota or a reason to suppress contextually supported matches. The evaluation records the applied 0.60 threshold, configured category ceilings and resulting selection decisions.

Quantify contextual correctness using source-linked reference judgments prepared independently of the model output: supported, contradicted or not established, and uncertain. Review false matches and missed supported qualities separately. Report the number judged and uncertain, supported-assignment rate among adjudicated assignments, and missed-supported rate within a fully adjudicated reference set. A selected-output review alone cannot measure recall; a small reference subset cannot establish full-vocabulary recall. Do not count missing source evidence as proof the quality is absent from the film. Keep source coverage and definition ambiguity visible. Cost is a separate measurement. This requires human semantic review to produce meaningful numbers; no enjoyment, narrative-importance or preference-quality score is part of this baseline.

Two decisions must not be conflated:

1. **Logical judgment:** evaluate a defined quality independently when several qualities can coexist. Supply a self-contained definition, useful exclusions and relevant film or expression context. Do not make qualities compete for one winner just because they share a category.
2. **Request packing:** independent questions can share a request. “One question per quality” does not mean “one HTTP request per quality”.

| Packing option | Useful property | Tradeoff / question to resolve |
| --- | --- | --- |
| One quality per request | Easy isolation and targeted reassessment | Repeats shared source context; extra round trips and failure handling |
| Several qualities per request | Shares context, with a bounded retry/failure unit | Batch size and grouping need justification; categories alone are not a semantic dependency |
| All relevant qualities in one request | Avoids repeated state and assembles one matching result | Must fit context, remain inspectable and define incomplete/failure handling |

TypeSafe recommends bundling questions that share state. Current official documentation describes state ingestion once, questions evaluated in parallel, and limits of 64k tokens per request and 32k for state plus each question, with no separate question-count cap. These capabilities support discussion; they do not establish an optimal Glutton batch size. [Fan-out guidance](https://docs.typesafe.ai/patterns/fan-out), [model limits](https://docs.typesafe.ai/models), [Noul criteria](https://docs.typesafe.ai/primitives/noul). Checked 28 October 2026.

Initial assignment captures the complete authoritative vocabulary, definitions/category ceilings and imported film source context. It sends one bundled support request, then supporting-paragraph Choice questions for selected qualities in batches of at most four. Code applies the 0.60 gate and category ceilings with stable ID tie-breaking. Exact questions, responses, probabilities, decisions, source snapshots and usage/cost are retained in FilmQualityEval. Requests are bounded with explicit failures, no retries and no long-context opt-in. Future reassessment and Ratings question design remain separately scoped. No further live experiment is authorised by this document.

The [retained findings](research/film_quality_findings.md) summarise feasibility, cost and limits. The [six uncapped examples](film_qualities/examples.json) preserve original source paragraphs/provenance, all 1,410 probabilities and the 318 matched-candidate review records. These are inspection examples, not accepted associations, validated preferences or a production threshold policy. Superseded tuning artifacts were removed at the owner's request.

### Initial-release scope: enrichment and admin improvement

Owner clarification, 29 October 2026: import films from TMDB, enrich them with Wikipedia plot/context and assign qualities within each category's configured maximum. Bulk seed and ordinary users' missing-film imports via TMDB search use the same canonical pipeline, without requiring manual review of every imported film. Deduplicate by TMDB identity; distinguish pending/failed source enrichment and assignment from completion. Import retries and source sync must not overwrite later admin quality corrections. Importing a canonical film does not itself create a personal Library entry.

The import is one Film knowledge process: search TMDB, select the specific film identity, then import canonical metadata and Wikipedia context together. Resolve TMDB's Wikidata identity to its English Wikipedia sitelink internally; callers do not prepare page IDs or run a separate enrichment operation. Success means the stored metadata and source context are ready for assessment against existing quality definitions. Fetch and validate both sources before creating a Film; missing identity/article/context or provider failure leaves no partial film. Import is create-only: an existing TMDB identity is an explicit conflict, including concurrent duplicates. Bulk and on-demand imports use the same operation. Updating metadata/Wikipedia context will be a separate explicit workflow using the same fetchers, preserving accepted qualities and admin corrections. Store provider identity in tmdb_id/wikidata_id and fetched inputs directly in tmdb_metadata/wikipedia_context. Source fields remain optional to allow explicit purging of TMDB or Wikipedia data; that does not make re-import an update/repair workflow. Quality assessment/assignment follows this import and remains a distinct step.

Implementation starts from the existing baseline: >=60% contextual support and individually configured category ceilings, with weights initially 1. Use deterministic within-category selection for initial seed assignment; no more policy research is required before building. Missing-source/readiness and retry behaviour belong in the bounded seed implementation spec, not a separate change-suggestion workflow. Import retries must preserve later manual corrections.

Delivery order:

1. **Load seed content:** persist/load existing quality definitions/vocabulary, import films with TMDB metadata/Wikipedia context and initial quality associations.
2. **View content, admin-only:** canonical film, category and quality lists/details; show source context, definitions, settings and current associations. Navigate film -> qualities -> category and category/quality -> associated records. Authorisation is required before exposure; editing is not required for inspection.
3. **Manual editing:** quality definition tuning within a concept (immutable names), category grouping/caps/weights and manual film-quality additions/removals, with explicit validation and freshness rules. Plot corrections stay on Wikipedia.
4. **Manual TMDB/Wikipedia refresh:** fetch current source data and report status/provenance, leaving accepted associations unchanged.
5. **JEV change evaluation:** connect the quality check to refresh, inspect additions/removals/evidence and explicitly apply selected changes. Preserve completed judgments in FilmQualityEval; suggested differences may be reconstructed from those results without retaining a separate suggestion record. No persistent FilmQualitySuggestion or mandatory review queue is required.
6. **Post-release expression investigation/testing:** examine viewer expressions for missing vocabulary, film links or category-preference coverage; test proposals before explicit changes. This does not defer core expression interpretation/scoring for personal preferences.

The admin screens are distinct from ordinary user TMDB import and personal Library management. Community editing remains deferred. Future admin access to Ratings-owned expressions requires appropriate authorisation/minimal context; read access does not transfer ownership or imply permission to publish text. The [workflow proposal](film_quality_review_workflow.md) follows this delivery order. Suggestions, tests and source sync do not themselves mutate accepted qualities or user scoring.

## Accepted film-quality changes and downstream effects

Proposed boundary from the owner discussion: a deliberate, accepted change to a film's associated qualities—initially through system assignment or deliberate admin correction—makes affected scoring eligible for reassessment. Fetching source text, editing a draft, requesting a JEV assessment and receiving candidate matches do not by themselves change accepted qualities.

No actual association change means no downstream scoring change. A category-only rename or move does not alter quality meaning and should not rescore by itself. A definition/meaning revision can require reassessing affected film and expression links, separately from an article refresh. Initial association assignment and deliberate admin changes need explicit apply/freshness semantics; public community editing is outside the initial release.

Film knowledge owns the vocabulary and accepted film associations. Ratings owns interpretation and replacement of derived rating-quality contributions. Recommendations owns recomputed recommendations and their saved contributions. Use the agreed durable asynchronous domain-event mechanism where other domains must react; agree event meaning, payload, ordering, delivery, duplicates, failure handling and retention before implementing publishers/subscribers in separate tasks. No event contract is invented by this document. Shared reads remain permitted.

Keep original ratings and expression text unchanged. Replace affected current derived contributions without double counting. Reject late work for superseded inputs and distinguish pending/stale results from completed current results. Profile-only changes still leave directly expressed evidence unaffected; semantic changes to that quality or its expression link are a separate reassessment reason. The [Ratings scoring design](ratings_scoring_design.md) remains authoritative for Ratings rules.

## Film quality evaluation evidence

Quality owns its matching criteria and QualityCategory owns category settings. Consumers read these properties through ordinary attribute readers; the evaluation workflow selects its input snapshot and checks that criteria are complete. It likewise reads Film source properties directly; Film exposes has_source_context? and has_film_qualities? state predicates; assignment and evaluation own their errors, snapshots and freshness checks.

Film knowledge owns FilmQualityEval. Initial assessment and future reassessment use immutable completed or failed evaluation records, separate from accepted FilmQuality associations. Preserve source and definition snapshots, exact ordered request/response pairs and call count, all support/evidence probabilities, model/question/policy identities, per-quality selection decisions and usage/cost. A single application outcome records accepted association IDs or a safe failure reason; recording an evaluation does not itself accept qualities. Stale or failed application retains the completed judgment without changing accepted associations. Zero-match evaluations are retained; Film.without_qualities remains the coverage query. Failed provider work retains a failed evaluation with partial answers and safe errors; it cannot produce accepted qualities. Calls blocked before sending are distinguished from attempted provider requests. Usage/cost totals cover validated responses and flag incomplete usage; transport/unparseable failures have no JSON response to retain.

FilmQualityEval applies its selected results to its own film and records the application outcome atomically, checking Film’s context and association state under the film lock. Generated FilmQuality records reference the evaluation that produced them. Their quality identity locates its specific judgment, and evidence remains in its output against its saved source revision; FilmQuality stores no duplicate evidence column or evidence reader. Manual/pre-existing associations may have no evaluation. These product records remain readable without Langfuse; trace IDs are optional diagnostic links. A future source purge must consider retained evaluation snapshots explicitly rather than implying that clearing current context erases them. No reassessment scheduler, source-purge operation, suggestion queue or generic evaluation lifecycle is implemented here.

## Historical score records

Rating qualities preserve which qualities, evidence/classifications and scores contributed to their historical rating assessments. Recommendation qualities preserve the actual contributions, totals and scoring-rule identity used when a recommendation was computed. Historical explanations read those saved results rather than rebuilding them from the film's current qualities or the user's current profile. A later calculation produces a new result; do not overwrite an earlier result or backdate its availability.

Ensure historical records still convey the quality meaning actually assessed. A mutable reference alone is insufficient if its meaning can be changed in place: choose stable concept identities, referenced meaning revisions or the necessary saved explanatory content when specifying scoring. This does not imply archiving every historical film-quality association set.

Quality is a shared vocabulary concept, QualityCategory owns grouping/configuration, Film owns current canonical metadata/source context, and FilmQuality is the accepted association to a Quality. FilmQualityEval retains assessed inputs and judgments independently. There is no FilmAnalysis/AnalysisQuality lifecycle or independent archive of every former association set. Creation migrations express the current catalogue directly.

Source syncing, source/evidence retention, admin changes and suggestion application still require their bounded operations. No storage migration or source refresh itself publishes scoring-change events or rewrites another domain's historical results. GitHub retains the former implementation and delivery history; maintained documents describe the current contract.

## Presentation

Expose useful source-backed film information and accepted qualities with appropriate spoiler labelling, evidence/context and a way to report inaccuracies. Source narrative, film traits and personal recommendation explanations have different responsibilities even if no independent analysis model remains. Do not invent predicted scene reactions beyond actual engine outputs. Direct access need not require Library watched status; normal watched-library navigation can carry a spoiler warning. Admin viewing precedes manual editors, then source refresh and JEV change inspection. Expression-led vocabulary discovery is post-release; any podcast comparison remains separately scoped. Community editing is deferred.
